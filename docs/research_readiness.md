# Research readiness: technical foundation

Verified at commit `1ab960e` + this milestone. Numbers below are from `python -m tests.research_report` (committed in `evaluation_results/research_report.{md,json}`); re-run it, do not copy them.

## 1. Formal contribution and invariants
An evidence-aware decision procedure for VEX justifications over SBOMs: a claim is judged by *evidence states* (Present, Absent, Unknown, Incomplete, Ambiguous, Contradictory), *effective provenance* (P0-P3) and a *rule table*, yielding exactly three verdicts (Supported, Weak, Insufficient) or a structured refusal. Invariants, each tested: P-1 sufficiency is not truth; P-2 missing evidence is Unknown, never Absent; P-3 monotone downgrade (a rule can only lower the ceiling; unresolved dimensions impose the Insufficient ceiling, addenda v0.2.2/v0.2.3); inputs are immutable and evaluation is deterministic; a caller can never supply a state, level, BIND result or verdict.

## 2. Predicates and rule coverage (synthetic conformance, `python -m tests.conformance`)
* `Req(component_not_present)`: N1-N17, NT1-NT25. * `Req(vulnerable_code_not_in_execute_path)`: R1-R13, RT1-RT13. Each rule is counted only if observed to trigger in a passing case (report: CNP 30 cases, 17/17 rules, 25/25 tests; reachability 42 cases, 13/13 rules, 13/13 tests). The analyzer corpus alone triggers 9/13 reachability rules (R7-R10 need strict-input absence/incomplete facts and are covered by the conformance cases).

## 4. Provenance and coverage assumptions
* **Source-to-assessed-artifact link (audit A).** Every analyzer-derived outcome depends on one assumption: the evaluator-entered `AnalysisSubject` (artifact purl/version, repository, revision, tree digest, target, root, entry methods) truly describes how the assessed artifact S relates to the analysed checkout. The system has no authoritative source for it (a built artifact is not cryptographically tied to a commit here), so it does not invent one: the document carries the subject's artifact *without hashes* (so BIND can match only by product/version, B4/B5), every evaluated outcome built on an artifact lists `SOURCE_TO_ARTIFACT_LINK_EVALUATOR_ASSERTED` (tested over the whole corpus), and a derived document is never P2/P3. Consequence: an evaluator who declares a false relationship can obtain, from an unauthenticated artifact + record, at most **Weak** (absence) or a lowering outcome (path); never Supported. Supported appears in the corpus only through a caller-supplied K1 report verified by a detached result. Mismatches among record, artifact and subject (repository, revision, tree digest, target, root, tool, output hash) yield BIND Unknown (R6) and never a stronger verdict (tests). The *evaluator* being dishonest is out of scope (threat to validity T1).
* **Negative K1 (audit B).** Absence is derived only when all 14 coverage facts are established, no path is recorded and nothing is undecidable; a successful process, an empty `results`, a missing `codeFlows`, schema validity or a bare declaration never suffice (mutation tests on every fact). The declared coverage claim is the *record author's* assertion (A6): the derived route is **not stronger** than a caller-authored strict-input K1 report with `full_coverage_claim` (also at most Weak without P3); what it adds are consistency gates between the claim and the artifact. Entry points: no tool enumerates them; completeness is a declaration (T3).
* **Run records (audit C).** Verified by tests: no record yields P2/P3 (also with a verified detached result, P3 needs P2 first); altered/absent output hash, dirty/untracked/changed trees, failed/timed-out stages and unresolved paths all leave the verdict equal or lower. One concrete defect was found and fixed: a record-derived absence still carried the limitation text "NO_ABSENCE_EVIDENCE ... no absence is derived" (contradicting the result); it is now removed when an absence is derived.

## 5. Evaluation methodology and results
Corpus: A = 9 upstream examples (byte-for-byte), B = 187 synthetic, L = 12 authentic CodeQL 2.27.3 cases (6 live runs, 3 with the runner's own record); 208 cases, all agree with hand-derived expectations. The report gives per category: formats, evaluated/refused, REACH/BIND states, verdicts, inconclusive cases with **one** primary reason each, coverage facts per record-case (denominator = evaluated cases with a record), negative K1 derivations (6 of 75 record cases, all Weak, all category B), and a **weakening analysis**: 82 record cases x 10 degradations of the record = 820 pairs; none yields a verdict above both the original and the no-record verdict (10 pairs return to the no-record verdict, case U12b: a derived lower-provenance K1 report lowers a verified one by the minimum-provenance rule, §1.2 step 5).

## 6. What the experiments do and do not establish
They establish that the implementation follows the written rule tables and the stated conservative policies on these inputs, deterministically, and that the checked invariants hold across the corpus. They do **not** establish detection accuracy, prevalence, the soundness or completeness of CodeQL/Joern, that the specification is right, or anything about real SBOMs/projects. No accuracy/precision/recall/FP/FN figure exists because no independently labelled ground truth exists. Category L shows integration behaviour on those artifacts only.

## 7. Threats to validity
T1 the evaluator-entered subject, locus, entry set and hazards are trusted statements; T2 expectations are my reading of the specification (single reviewer); T3 entry-point completeness and coverage claims are declarations; T4 corpus is tiny and mostly synthetic (187 of 208 hand-built); T5 no authentic Joern output; T6 min-provenance aggregation is non-monotone in *added* evidence (documented); T7 conventions AI-D*, AR-D*, G15-D* are unconfirmed; T8 tools modelled at one version (CodeQL 2.27.3); T9 symlink/case-folding path issues are assumed away (roots canonical).
